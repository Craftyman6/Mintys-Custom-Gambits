# Minty's Custom Gambits
Mods for Gambonanza that add custom gambits to the game through the usage of the [Gambonanza Mod Manager](https://bentrd.github.io/GambonanzaMods/).
## Roadside Pack
- **Stop Sign's Gambit** Waiting skips the enemy turn
- **U-Turn's Gambit** Moving a piece backwards blesses it
- **One Way's Gambit** Moving a piece to the right protects it
- **Speed Limit's Gambit** Moving a piece 1 tile gives $1. Moving a piece 3 or more tiles costs $3
- **Road Work Ahead's Gambit** Capturing an enemy piece <shake>crumbles</shake> the tile ahead of it.
- **Golden Road's Gambit** Promoting into a king turns every tile golden, fills your stock with kings, gives Dainsleif's Gambit, and destroys this gambit.
